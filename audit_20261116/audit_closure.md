## Reconciled denominators and results

- E1: 149 station-months × 3 = 447 component slots; 420 have an unmasked S8 truth candidate, 27 report the original `RuntimeError: Complete-data S8 has NO_PASS candidate; E1 relative target undefined`. They were not quietly discarded. The authenticated compressed full case archive has 555,660 mask-method cases with station, month, component, nominal and realized mask fraction, replicate, mask seed, status, and error; 514,438 are OK, 30,602 NO_MATRIX, and 10,620 NO_PASS. `case_manifest.csv` indexes truth slots, while `results/phase3/F3_E1_holdout_cases.csv.gz` is the full E1 case table.
- U3: 420 truth-defined slots × the first five mask replicates = 2,100 per setting and method. The manifest preserves nominal and realized fractions.
- E2: 8,748 manifest cases = 3,132 development + 5,616 holdout. The holdout has 2,808 unmasked cases; 247 fail A1, leaving 2,561 eligible for post-hoc matched comparison. Frozen A1+A2 accepts 2,083/2,808 = 74.18%, below 80%.
- E3: 1,068 cases and 830 accepted are **combined** E3 primary (570/426) and E3b exploratory (498/404). They should not be presented as 1,068 primary cases.
- U5 band check: at least 22 of 24 target hours lie in the pointwise band for 301/440, 678/746 and 867/897 accepted cases at alpha 0.5, 1 and 2; station-month cluster 95% intervals are 61.8–74.4%, 87.9–93.9%, and 94.9–98.2%. The band is conditional variability, not a simultaneous confidence band.

## Post-hoc matched acceptance and null

The four ablation arms use identical status/A1/completeness eligibility. No-A2 accepts all 2,561 eligible cases (median error 0.0590, p90 0.4766). At exactly 2,083 accepted, frozen stability gives median 0.04623 and p90 0.21520; selected-candidate absolute correlation ranking gives 0.04595 and 0.21637. Station-month cluster 95% intervals for full minus correlation are `[-0.00176, 0.00190]` (median) and `[-0.03342, 0.03196]` (p90): **no clear incremental benefit of stability over correlation ranking**. Across 1,000 random matched rejections, median-of-medians is 0.05899 and median p90 is 0.47798. Cluster intervals for full minus random are `[-0.01612, -0.00990]` and `[-0.42298, -0.14417]`. The descriptive correlation cutoff `|r| = 0.91554` came from the holdout; it is not a validated deployment cutoff. `ablation_scores.csv` verifies the recomputed archived candidate error to `1e-10` in each of 2,561 eligible cases. See `ablation_cases.csv`, `ablation_random_replicates.csv`, `ablation_cluster_bootstrap.csv`, risk CSV and figure.

For the new paired null, self-reference candidate-only passes 57/100 (Wilson 95% CI 47.2–66.3%), and full A1+A2 accepts 16/100 (10.1–24.4%), with 41 pass-to-reject transitions. Independent-reference candidate-only passes 41/100 (31.9–50.8%), full accepts 13/100 (7.8–21.0%), with 28 transitions. The same case bank is used within each stratum. This AR(1) null is limited to 30 independent days of hourly rho 0.9, 5 nT marginal noise, seven candidates, and the specified reference constructions. It provides no universal false-positive guarantee.

## Bootstrap and DIFI checks

The frozen E2 bootstrap holds the IQD reference and its 1,000-surrogate set fixed over 200 day resamples. All 200 contribute to stability, including no-selection draws. Only successful selected curves enter the hourly 5th and 95th percentiles. Seeds derive from the case ID; the band is pointwise and conditional on imputation and reference. The SSB daily lognormal factors are divided by their within-month mean so the injected monthly mean is exactly alpha times the DIFI target.

The archived provenance and local coefficients verify pyDIFI 8.1.5, `model_name=DIFI8`, DIFI8 coefficient SHA-256 `69ac93ed533e6654af7582942cab90c8e200c93fc442ae80300f7c246e7e34bf`, F10.7 SHA-256 `b62b2605522a47a977d64c2e82a6a55461e281db4544a77310579a9c2317577a`, WGS84 height 0 km, local-hour UTC HH:30, and primary-plus-induced XYZ. Excluding the 10 GUA and 9 KDU pre-2014 UTC samples from each affected monthly hourly average changes any target hour by at most 0.093 nT, relative target L2 by at most 0.00204, and accepted E3 correlation by at most 0.00037. This is a narrow boundary sensitivity, not evidence of independent DIFI truth.
